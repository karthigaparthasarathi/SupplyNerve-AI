# SupplyNerve AI

SupplyNerve AI is an AI-powered Supply Chain Risk Intelligence Platform that helps procurement and planning teams proactively identify supply disruptions before they impact business operations.

## Problem Statement

Supply chain teams often face challenges such as:

- Forecast volatility
- Supplier response delays
- Demand fluctuations
- Limited visibility into supply risks
- Manual exception monitoring

These challenges can lead to shortages, delays, and operational disruptions.

## Solution

SupplyNerve AI continuously analyzes:

- Forecast changes
- Supplier commitments
- Demand fluctuations
- Supply constraints

The platform identifies critical exceptions and provides AI-powered recommendations to support proactive decision-making.

## Key Features

- Forecast Change Analysis
- Supplier Response Tracking
- Risk Classification
- AI-Powered Recommendations
- Supply Chain Risk Monitoring
